Our Story

Handcrafted with Heart

A Floral Experience was founded on a simple belief: flowers are more than decoration — they are emotion made visible. Our studio in Irving, TX brings together skilled designers, locally sourced blooms, and a deep passion for celebrating life's milestones.

  • Wedding florals from intimate ceremonies to grand receptions
  • Corporate event design and branded installations
  • Sympathy and memorial arrangements, handled with care
  • Same-day delivery throughout the DFW Metroplex
  • Seasonal subscriptions for home and office
