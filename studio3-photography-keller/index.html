Our Story

Rooted in Keller,
Reaching DFW

Studio 3 Photography has been a cornerstone of the Keller community for over 15 years. Founded on the belief that every person deserves to see themselves beautifully, we've grown from a single-camera operation into one of DFW's most trusted photography studios.

We specialize in portraits, events, and commercial work — bringing the same level of craft, care, and artistry to every project regardless of size. Our light-filled Keller studio is designed to be a warm, welcoming place where great photos happen naturally.
