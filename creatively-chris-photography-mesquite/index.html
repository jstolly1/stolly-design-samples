Meet Christine

Photography rooted in love

Hi, I'm Christine — a Mesquite, TX-based photographer with a deep belief that the best photos aren't posed, they're discovered. For over eight years I've been documenting the in-between moments: the nervous laugh before the vows, the first yawn of a sleeping newborn, the way a family looks at each other when no one's watching.

My approach is relaxed, intentional, and always personal. I want you to feel comfortable, not perform for a camera. That's when the real images happen.
