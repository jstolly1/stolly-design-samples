01 / The Craft

Three trades, one finish.

01 — Tile & Coping

Waterline tile set true.

Glass, stone, and ceramic mosaics laid level along the waterline and coping — the detail every guest notices first and the surface that takes the hardest wear.

  • Glass & ceramic mosaic waterline tile
  • Stone & precast coping
  • Step, spa, and raised-wall accents
  • Tile repair & matching on older pools

02 — Plaster & Finishes

The surface you touch.

From classic white plaster to quartz and pebble finishes, troweled smooth for a surface that feels right underfoot and holds its color season after season.

  • White, colored & quartz plaster
  • Pebble & aggregate finishes
  • Smooth hand-troweled application
  • Acid-wash & finish detailing

03 — Resurfacing

An old pool, made new.

Chip-out, re-tile, and re-plaster — a full resurfacing that returns a tired pool to its first day. The work that turns 1986 craftsmanship into a 2026 backyard.

  • Full chip-out & re-plaster
  • Complete tile & coping replacement
  • Color & finish upgrades
  • Remodels & transformations
