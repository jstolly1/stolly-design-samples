02 — Provenance

A hundred years of discretion.

Noir Vesper has never advertised. Our archive is told in acquisitions, not announcements.

1924

The First Vault

Founded above a clockmaker's in the second arrondissement, the atelier opens with a single commission and a standing rule: no piece leaves the room until the buyer has seen it by candlelight.

1961

Into Stone & Mortar

An estate offered as collateral becomes our first address. The house quietly begins to broker the rarest private residences in Europe alongside its jewels.

1998

The Vesper Cut

Our master cutter patents a 71-facet brilliant designed to hold low light — the cut still bears the house name and is reserved for archive pieces.

Today

By Invitation

Four ateliers, eleven clients per year, and a waiting list measured in seasons. Access remains the rarest material we work in.
