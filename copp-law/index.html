The Firm

A practice built for the long arc of a business.

Most entities are formed in a hurry and regretted at leisure. We do the opposite — we form them to last.

Since 2009, The Copp Law Firm has counseled Texas entrepreneurs, investors, and licensed professionals through every stage of the corporate life cycle: the careful formation, the elections that follow, the conversions that growth demands, and the quiet annual maintenance that keeps an entity in good standing year after year.

We are deliberately Texas-focused and attorney-handled. There are no account managers between you and your counsel, and no template churned out by a filing mill — only structure built around your business, your liability, and your plans.

The Attorney

Zachary "Zac" Copp

Founding Attorney · The Copp Law Firm, PC

For more than two decades, Zac Copp has practiced business law in Texas with a single conviction: that the way a company is formed shapes everything that follows. Licensed for over 23 years and founder of the firm since 2009, he has personally guided the formation of more than 3,750 Texas LLCs since 2015.

His clients are founders, investors, and licensed professionals who want their counsel on the phone — not a portal and a queue.
