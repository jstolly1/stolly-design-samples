What We Do

Every Surface. Every Material.

From living room furniture to classic cars to lake boats — if it can be upholstered, we do it. Three decades of experience, one shop in Mesquite.

Furniture Re-Upholstery

Breathe new life into your sofas, chairs, ottomans, and dining sets. We work with your fabric or help you choose from our extensive selection.

  • Sofas & sectionals
  • Dining & accent chairs
  • Ottomans & benches
  • Custom cushions & pillows
  • Antique & heirloom restoration

Auto Upholstery

Classic restorations, custom interiors, and factory-quality repairs for cars, trucks, and SUVs. We match OEM specs or build something entirely custom.

  • Seat re-upholstery
  • Door panel replacement
  • Headliner repair & replacement
  • Convertible tops
  • Classic car restoration

Marine Upholstery

UV-resistant, marine-grade fabrics built to withstand sun, salt, and water. We keep your boat seats looking sharp through every season on the lake.

  • Boat seat cushions
  • Pontoon furniture
  • Ski boat interiors
  • Bimini tops & covers
  • Marine vinyl repair

Our Craft

Handcrafted the Old-Fashioned Way

We don't cut corners. Every piece that comes through our shop at 333 Gross Rd gets the same attention it would have gotten in 1990 when we opened — proper spring tying, hand-cut fabric, precision stitching. The difference is visible. And it lasts.

We work with leather, vinyl, fabric, canvas, and specialty marine materials. Bring us your fabric or choose from our wide selection of in-stock options.
