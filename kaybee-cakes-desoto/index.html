Our Story

Meet Kathy Boyd,
Baker & Creator

Kathy Boyd has been turning flour, butter, and imagination into edible memories for over two decades. What started as weekend baking for family gatherings grew into KayBee Cakes Bakery — a DeSoto institution known for cakes that look as extraordinary as they taste.

Every cake that leaves Kathy's kitchen is made from scratch with premium ingredients and finished by hand. No shortcuts, no mass-produced decorations. Just real artistry, real flavors, and real care for each customer's vision.

Whether you need a towering five-tier wedding showpiece or a dozen cupcakes for a Tuesday birthday, Kathy brings the same passion and attention to every single order.

— Kathy Boyd
