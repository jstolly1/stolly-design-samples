What Peekaboo Pediatric Dentistry does

Gentle, kid-focused dentistry from your child's very first tooth through the teenage years — all in one welcoming Prosper office.

Preventive

Cleanings, Exams & Sealants

Routine checkups, cleanings, fluoride, sealants, and silver diamine fluoride to keep little smiles healthy and cavity-free.

Restorative

Fillings & Crowns

Tooth-colored fillings, crowns, and pulpotomies to gently restore teeth and keep your child comfortable.

Comfort

Sedation Dentistry

Oral conscious sedation and general anesthesia options so anxious children can get the care they need calmly and safely.

Infant Care

Lip & Tongue Tie Treatment

Laser frenectomy and lip- and tongue-tie care to support comfortable feeding and healthy development.

Surgical

Extractions & Oral Surgery

Gentle extractions and minor oral surgery handled with care to keep your child relaxed and reassured.
