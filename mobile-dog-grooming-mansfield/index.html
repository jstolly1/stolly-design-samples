Our Services

01

Full Grooming Package

Bath, blow-dry, breed-specific haircut, ear cleaning, nail trim, and finishing spritz. Head-to-tail perfection, door to door.

02

Bath & Brush-Out

Premium shampoo, conditioner, thorough blow-dry, and a full de-shedding brush-out. Ideal for maintaining a healthy, shiny coat between full grooms.

03

Nail Trim & Grinding

Precision nail trimming and optional grinding for smooth edges. Gentle technique keeps even anxious dogs calm and safe.

04

Puppy’s First Groom

A slow, positive introduction to grooming for puppies 8–16 weeks. We build confidence early so grooming stays stress-free for life.

05

Obedience Training

Private in-home sessions covering sit, stay, leash manners, and impulse control. Positive reinforcement only — no punishment, no fear.

06

De-Shedding Treatment

Specialized shampoo, conditioner, and high-velocity blow-out dramatically reduces shedding for weeks. A must for double-coated breeds.
