Grace's Tiers opened in 2016 on a simple idea: a real bakery, where one person still tastes the batter and signs off on every order that goes out the door. No mixes, no shortcuts, no day-old case dressed up as fresh.

That is why people describe walking in as feeling like family, and why the custom orders book out. When you order from Grace, you are ordering from Grace.
