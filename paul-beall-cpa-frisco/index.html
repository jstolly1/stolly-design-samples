What We Do

Comprehensive tax and accounting services

From annual tax returns to complex IRS matters, we handle every layer of your financial picture with precision and care.

Individual Tax Preparation

Accurate, thorough federal and state returns for individuals and families — including complex situations like rental income, investments, and self-employment.

Business & Corporate Tax

Business entity returns for S-Corps, C-Corps, LLCs, and partnerships. We maximize deductions and keep your business fully compliant.

Tax Planning & Strategy

Proactive planning so you're never surprised at year-end. We identify opportunities — retirement contributions, timing strategies, entity structuring — before they expire.

IRS Audit Representation

Face the IRS with confidence. We represent you directly — communicating with auditors, responding to notices, and protecting your rights every step of the way.

QuickBooks Setup & Support

Get your books in order from day one. We set up, clean up, and train you on QuickBooks so your financial records support smart decisions year-round.

Payroll Services

Timely, accurate payroll processing with full compliance — federal and state withholding, quarterly filings, and W-2 preparation handled for you.

About Paul Beall

A trusted advisor, not just a tax preparer.

Paul Beall, CPA has spent his career helping Frisco families and small businesses navigate the complexities of the tax code. He believes your accountant should be someone you call all year long — not just in April.

With a background in both public accounting and private industry, Paul brings a practical perspective: minimize what you legally owe, maximize what you keep, and eliminate surprises. His clients range from individual households to multi-entity business owners across the Dallas–Fort Worth area.
