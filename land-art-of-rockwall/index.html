Land Art was established in 1988 and is family owned and operated. Since then, the team has provided Rockwall homeowners with quality work in lawn care, irrigation, landscape design, and stonework.

Owner Ron Hawkins and the crew bring around 50 years of combined experience in landscape design, irrigation, drainage, and hardscapes — and recently opened a retail location, Honey Locust Farms, to help customers care for what they plant.
